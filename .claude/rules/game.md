# Game shell and UI (`src/game`, `src/lab`)

`docs/DESIGN.md` describes the mechanics and controls; ADR 0014 sets the pillars.

- Controls: mouse plus keyboard, at most 6 core actions (ADR 0014): steer, dash, court, attack, the social wheel and the menu (DESIGN §3). Each has a keyboard equivalent and a visible hint. Any other key (chat, emote, pond map, promote, the combat keys) is a shortcut for something the screen also offers, so the game plays with the six alone. Combat keys work only in combat.
- Readable consequences: every source of damage and every death cause has a visible cue (audible from M6). The death screen always names the cause. Never a silent death.
- The core loop (move, feed, survive, find a mate) must work without opening any genetics UI. Genetics adds depth; it never gates the fun.
- No NPC organisms, fake players or bots in the live world. Test clients and simulated populations never point at the production authority.
- Test hook `window.__bs`: read-only views and replay are always present. Mutating helpers exist only under `?debug=1` and never change authority-side state.
- Keep text short and plain English. Never use colour as the only signal (threat outlines also change shape or pulse).
- Colour never alone (M14 T9, `src/game/cue-channels.ts`): a new cue that carries meaning joins `CUES` with the colours it draws with (each the literal its file writes, which `cue-channels.test.ts` finds there) and a channel that isn't colour and holds with motion reduced and the sound off (shape, pattern, icon, text or position). Two colours that mean different things side by side join `CONFUSABLE`: they keep a CIEDE2000 distance of 12 under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (`colour-vision.ts`), or the pair names the channel that tells them apart and the test lists it. Moving a cue colour means changing its literal in the inventory too. The bars' glyphs (a cross, a bolt, outward arrows, an hourglass) are `data-glyph` in `index.html`; a hit cracks the cross (`.crack` under `data-hurt`) and low energy hollows the bolt.
- The play screen is minimal (M6b, owner, Playtest #183): one menu button, four stat bars and the minimap (at most 6% of the screen), with no permanent text; the numbers live in the menu (Esc). Events (toasts, chips, hints, the result card) may appear and go. Damage and hazards never paint a border, vignette or overlay: damage lands as distinct hits at most every 0.5 s that gently jolt the body and kick the camera (no kicks under Reduce motion), flash the body deep red and the health bar, and sound; the hazard icon shows beside the minimap's depth gauge. The wheel and the menu never stop the player's movement. Nothing covers the egg while it waits, on the title and death screens too.
- The first-life guide (M14 T7, `src/game/guide.ts`): `Guide` is pure and takes the loop's clock (`App.virtualNow`) and each frame's `GuideFacts` (`PlayUi.guideFacts`); a step is a row of `RULES` (where, when, until, the longest show, `urgent` for the three that show at once). A new action worth teaching joins as a step there, with its words in `GUIDE_WORDS` (one line at 390 px) and DESIGN §10. The line is `hint`: it never takes the pointer and covers none of the bars, the minimap, the offspring card or the egg.
- How to play (M14 T8): `CONTROLS` (`src/game/controls.ts`) is the one table of controls. A key the game starts handling joins its handler's key list (`APP_KEYS` in `app-keys.ts`, `MOVE_KEYS`, `FIGHT_KEYS`, `WHEEL_KEYS`, `EMOTE_KEYS`) and a `CONTROLS` row, or `controls.test.ts` fails. The panel's words live in `help-text.ts`: plain, short lines, no genetics words in How to play, and no number `docs/DESIGN.md` doesn't hold (`help-panel.test.ts`); change them with the mechanic. `Help` is pure over a `HelpView`; `?` and Esc reach it first in `attachKeys`, and its own keydown lets every other key through, so steering and the dash work with focus inside it.
- Toasts: `Hud.toast` shows one line for its time; `setLife(null)` (a practice cell, every tick) leaves it, and a new life or a death clears it (`clearToast`). Crowd instances (M12 T6): a creature in other water fades over 1 s (`RemoteSet.fadeOut`, wired in `app-online.ts`), and `debug.crowdAt` sets the page's `CROWD_AT` before or after its link starts (`App.crowdAt`).
- Riding the current (M12 T7, `src/game/ride.ts`): `Ride` is pure and takes the time it is given. The offer shows once the page has been alone (`OnlineSession.alone()`, playing and alive) for `aloneMs` plus the report's 2 s gap, for 12 s and again every 2 minutes; it takes the alone hint's place (`ride-offer`, whose button takes the pointer), and the menu's `menu-ride` shows while the page is alone. R or either button sends one `ride` (`OnlineWiring.askRide`). On the hub's `spawn { why: 'ride' }` the transition plays on wall time with input held (`Ticker.chooseInput`): the first half sweeps the sim creature from where it was toward the arrival, the turn puts it a few radii short of the arrival and snaps the camera, zones and field there (`Ticker.carry`), the second half drifts it in, ending exactly at the arrival; under Reduce motion it holds still and turns at once (0.4 s). While it plays, `creatureNow()` reports the arrival, so no report from the old place reaches the hub's anchor. The look is `src/render/ride-fx.ts` (streaks as one additive `LineSegments` and a water-coloured wash under the creatures: two draw calls while riding, none otherwise), fed from `Ride.look`. A new life resets it.
- The admission line (M13 T10, `src/game/queue-card.ts`): the words are pure (`cardWords`, `lineText`, `waitText`, `reopenText`, which takes the local offset at the reopening, so tests pass a fixed one) and `QueueCard` draws them on the title and death screens; `Screens.setQueue` (each frame from `OnlineWiring.updateNetUi`, from `HubClient.queue`) shows the card and makes Hatch and the fallback choices wait (`Screens.waiting`, which `hatch()` honours too). A practice cell begun in line swims in a local world (`AppLife.practice`), and its chip follows the hub rather than reading offline (`OnlineWiring.lineLife`); the seat's toast fires once when the line gives way to `online`.
- What the browser offers (M14 T6, `src/game/capability.ts`): each page's `main.ts` checks `capabilities(window)` before it starts anything; `unsupportedReason` sends the game and the Lab to the plain page without WebGL2 or module workers (the card only without module workers: it draws the flat preview), and `playLimits` decides how the game plays with less (`App.limits`: offline practice cells without WebSocket or IndexedDB, no links and no referee duty without RTCPeerConnection), with the title's `capability-note` saying why. A new browser API the game can't do without joins `Capabilities` and its unit tests.
- Interrupted transactions (M14 T4, ADR 0076): the page keeps no journal; it resumes from the hub's word. `LifeFlow` takes a `commit` or `genesis` for the player's own genesis in any state with no recorded life on screen (`genesisInFlight`; `hatch()` then waits for it and asks nothing new), keeps `life.mating` and names both in `view().inFlight`; `MateFlow` turns a `life.mating` into `awaiting` without owing the cost (the hub's condition holds it). `LifeStarts.awaitGenesis` (each frame) plays a genesis in flight as its own hatch; a resume of a never-reported genesis creature toasts `HATCHED_AWAY`; `missedDeath` (`src/game/missed-death.ts`) opens a fresh page with no creature and a fallback on the newest past life's death screen. `m14-interrupt` crashes the renderer at each step.
