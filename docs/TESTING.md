# Testing reference

The test hooks' API, the budget scenes and the hub ports. The rules for tests are in `.claude/rules/tests.md`; this is the reference they point to. Read the parts your test uses, and update this file in the same PR when you change a hook or add a hub.

## The game (`window.__bs`)

- `window.__bs` (`src/game/debug.ts`): `ready`, `state()` (screen, `zone` and `depth` under the player (null on the title; M6 T16), quality (`setting`, `detected` the preset auto resolves to, `bench` `pending|running|measured|stored|skipped`: automation is `skipped`, a stored result for this GPU `stored`), tick, player, death (`cause`, `survivedSeconds`, `peakBiomass`, `body`, and `by`, the killer's name when the hub settled a kill; M7 T13), life (the recorded life: `status` is the client state machine `idle|requesting|awaiting|verifying|playing|dead|practice`, plus `creature`, `genome`, `activeMs`, `lifespanMs`, `awaitingBeacon`, `lastAckN`, `ackedBiomass` (the biomass in the report the hub last took), `genesisIn`, `practice`, `stage`, `remainingMs`, `limitMs`), mate (`state`, `text` the line on screen, `invite`, `visited` states, `ready`), offspring (`kind`, `creature`, `species`, `rarity`, `generation`, `genomeHash`, `cardShown`, `eggShown`, `options` the ids of the title and death choices), remotes (each with `radius`, `genomeHash` and `species`, null while the tinted cell stands in), pond, social, net (`dropped` counts genome events ignored, `signed` the peers whose verified signed snapshot is held, `badSignatures` the signed snapshots that failed to verify; M7 T7), encounter (the current or last refereed fight, null before one, M7 T10: `id`, own `slot`, `frame` simulated here, `confirmed` frames covered by the referee's bundles from frame 0, `over` once `enc-end` arrived, `hash` the local `hashEncounter` then, `rollbacks`, `resyncs`, `desyncs`, and `fighters` by slot with `player`, `creature`, simulated `x`, `y`, `heading`, `health`, `maxHealth`, `stance`, `windup` (ticks left or null), `target` (the windup's target code or null), `braced`, `egg` (`x`, `y`, `health`, `eaten`, or null) and `outcome` (`in` while fighting)); M7 T11: `encounter.endHash` is the referee's hash from `enc-end` (null before it), each of `remotes` has `protected` and `fighting` (its snapshot's marks), and `attack` is `{ hint, shimmer, waiting }`: the remote the "F" glyph marks (the nearest this player could engage now) or null, the remotes drawn with the protection shimmer, and whether an engage awaits its answer; `player` includes `speed`, `elder`, `genomeHash`, `species`, `rarity`, `tier` and the sim's `stats`).
- Also on `window.__bs`: `events()` (counts since hatching), `replay(log)` (hash of an input log), `replayEncounter(log)` (an encounter log's final `hash` and `results`; `m7-determinism.spec.ts` replays every `src/sim/__golden__/encounter-*.json` in Chromium, M7 T10), `render.info()` (`remoteCells` fallback cells, `remoteBodies` genome bodies; `preset`, `pixelRatio`, `particles`; the post chain's `passes`, `targets`, `targetBytes`, `renderScale` and `dof` (`none|half|full`); the player body's `lod` (`high|medium|low`, null without a body) and mesh `vertices` (M6 T10); `caustic` (the caustics' strength at the camera) and `rays` (the god rays' strength drawn, always 0 on low; M6 T17); `outlines` (`{ bigger, smaller }`, the size outlines drawn, M6 T21); `calls` and `triangles` count the whole frame, every post pass included), `render.outlines()` (the size outlines as last drawn, M6 T21: `id`, `kind` `bigger|smaller`, CSS-pixel centre `x`, `y` and `radius`, `pulse` 0..1, always 0 for `smaller`), `render.specks(layer)` (`far|mid|near`: each on-screen speck's `index`, CSS-pixel `x`, `y`, `size`, depth `z`, CoC `blur` and `bright` (one of high's bright specks, M6 T17), as last drawn).
- With `?debug=1` also `debug.{setEnergy, setBiomass, setHealth, teleport, setZone, nearestFood(kind?), steerTo, spawnPlume, setRemotes, joinPond, deliver, hasFood, timeScale, freezeAt, resetWater, setClock, setBench}`; `freezeAt(tick, x, y)` freezes the world (as `timeScale(0)`) at a sim tick with the player stopped at a place and the camera on it, so a shot does not depend on how many ticks a slow runner played first (`m6-dof`); `setZone(zone)` teleports the player to the nearest point well inside that zone (clear of its edge bands, its surround in the zone too) and returns it (M6 T16); freeze the world first (`timeScale(0)`) where UV or pressure would hurt; `setBench(ms)` replaces every measured frame time with `ms` and re-runs the auto-detect benchmark at once (8 high, 15 medium, 30 low), and adaptive resolution follows it, which is otherwise pinned at 1 under automation (low folds `renderScale` into `pixelRatio`); `steerTo` returns a promise, and works in a fight; its `id` may name a remote player, whose drawn body it follows until touching it (M7 T11); `setHealth(hp)` sets the player's health on this client only, never above its maximum, and peers and the hub see it in the snapshots; `timeScale(0)` also holds the shaders' animation clock, so specks stand still for screenshots; `resetWater()` zeroes the marine snow's flow and, once frozen, the shaders' clock (M6 T17), so two frozen pages hold the same specks. `setClock(seconds)` sets a frozen world's shaders' clock and leaves the flow, and the caustics' and god rays' clock (`SunClock`) re-bases to it at the camera's pace, so a frame at a set clock is the same on every run (M6b T8, `m6b-water.spec.ts`: each zone measured from the pond's centre at clock 100). `?dof=0` draws every preset without its depth of field and `?light=0` without caustics or god rays (test switches).
- A scripted remote (`setRemotes`) may carry a `genome` (any text, delivered through the real parser and builder; `withholdGenome` sends only the ref). The game hatches a random genesis body unless `?genome=<base64url>` or `?genome=starter` pins it; `openGame` in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts` adds `genome=starter` unless the query pins one. Tests click Hatch first: the page opens on the title screen, and `state().player` is `null` there.
- `state().social` holds `chat` (the last 50 lines), `muted` (player ids), `emotes`, `pings` and `dropped`; E2E chats through the real UI (Enter, type, Enter; click a roster name to mute), so there are no chat helpers under `?debug=1`.
- `state().egg` (M6 T20): the reveal's `phase` (`none|waiting|stirring|cracking|revealed`; M6b T11 added `stirring`) and `phases` (the last 16 entered, so a fast flow can be checked in order), `carried` (a pending child), `shown` (the player's egg drawn this frame), `peers` (ids drawn with an egg), `ghost` (a hatched parent still dissolving) and `motion` (`fade` under `prefers-reduced-motion`); `state().remotes[].egg`, `render.info().eggs` and `render.fx()` `bursts` and `cost`. The egg is drawn in the scene (no DOM element); its clock holds still under `timeScale(0)`. A scripted remote may carry `egg`. `m6-egg` has its own hub on 8798 with the M5 hooks (`M6_EGG_HUB`). M6b T11: `angle` (radians, the lean included, read at the call's time) and `glow` (0..1) of the player's egg, `at` (its CSS-pixel centre `x`, `y` and half-height `r`, null while it is not drawn), and `card` (`{ shown, tier }`: the genesis's result card `reveal-card`, else the offspring card); `render.eggAngle(ms)` is the swing at a time on the egg's clock (`performance.now()` while the world runs), since a busy software-rendered page draws too seldom to catch a wobble's peak. The DOM has `reveal-card` (`data-shown` while it shows, `data-tier`; `reveal-preview`, `reveal-name`, `reveal-stars` with its `aria-label`, `reveal-rarity`, `reveal-shape`), `offspring-stars` and `egg-wait`. A genesis's beacon comes 1 to 6 s after the commitment, sooner on a busy hub, so `m6b-egg.spec.ts` times its checks in the page; it has its own hub on 8801 with the M5 hooks (`M6B_EGG_HUB`). M6c T11: `state().egg` adds `charge` (0..1), `hint` (`cycling` before the beacon or while the tier is unknown, then the tier; null with no egg), `paced` (a genesis or a hatch waits for the player's crack), `beaconAt` and `crackAt` (ms on the egg's clock, null before), `crackedBy` (`player|auto|null`), `still` (the still scene holds) and `dim` (its fade, 0..1); `render.info().veil` is how much the veil darkens the picture (0 to 0.35) and `render.fx()` has `shockwaves` (full-charge bursts). A paced egg waits up to 6 s after the beacon, so `waitPlaying(page)` presses Enter (in the page) as soon as it stirs on the title or death screen; `waitPlaying(page, { paced: true })` leaves the crack to the test or the egg's wait. A hatch with H waits too: a spec presses Enter after H (Enter cracks it, and does not open the chat while the egg is still). `m6c-egg.spec.ts` has its own hub on 8805 with the M5 hooks (`M6C_EGG_HUB`).
- `state().audio` (M6 T22): `state` (`idle` before the first gesture, then the `AudioContext`'s `running|suspended|closed`), `cues` (counts per cue asked for since hatching, audible or not), `volume`, `muted`, `gain` (the master gain's target, 0 when muted), `zone` and `ambience` (the ambience's zone and parameter targets: `air`, `airHz`, `body`, `bodyHz`, `drone`, `droneHz`, `swellHz`, `shimmer`, `pressure`), `voices` and `placed` (the last placed cue's `cue`, `pan` and `gain`, M6 T23). `__bs.audio.render({ zone, depth?, seed, seconds, volume?, muted? })` renders the same graph on an `OfflineAudioContext` and returns `rmsDb`, `peakDb`, `silent`, `brightness`, `hash` and `length` (`m6-audio-render.spec.ts`); `__bs.audio.renderCue({ cue, seconds?, volume?, muted?, place? })` renders one cue alone, 0.5 s into the render so the limiter has settled, and adds `seconds` (its pinned length), `tail` (when it last rises above −60 dBFS) and each channel's linear RMS `left` and `right`. `debug.deliver` passes chat, emotes and pings through `Social` too; a scripted remote's id counts as in the roster. Headless Chromium runs an `AudioContext` without a speaker; E2E hides a tab by redefining `document.hidden` and `visibilityState` and dispatching `visibilitychange`. `state().egg.motion` follows the Reduce motion setting, else `prefers-reduced-motion` (`page.emulateMedia`).
- `state().wheel` (M6 T24): `open`, `items` (each `name`, `kind` `ping|emote`, `id`, `key`, and its CSS-pixel centre `x`, `y`; empty while closed), `at` (the world point a ping would land on), `centre` and `target` (the wheel's centre and the cursor it opened at, CSS pixels). The DOM has `wheel` and `wheel-item` (`data-kind`, `data-id`, `data-name`, `data-lit` under the pointer). Ping and emote icons (M6 T25) are `svg.icon` with `data-icon` (the item's name), `role="img"` and a `<title>`; while `T` waits for its number the chat shows `emote-strip` with six `emote-choice`; `m6-icons.spec.ts` saves the icon sheet (`test-results/m6-icons-sheet.png`). A tap of `G` leaves it open; Playwright holds it with `keyboard.down('g')`. `m6-wheel` has its own hub on 8799 (`M6_WHEEL_HUB`).
- `state().indicators` (M6 T19): the edge indicators as drawn (`kind` `player|ping`, `id`, `name`, `ping`, `angle` the world bearing from the player in degrees, `lengths`, `size` `smaller|comparable|bigger`, `ready`, CSS-pixel `x`, `y`); `state().map` (`open`, `players`, `pings`). The DOM has `edge-indicator` (`data-kind`, `data-id`, `data-size`, `data-ready`), `pond-map`, `map-frame` and `map-dot` (`data-id`, `data-self`, `data-ready`; measure its `svg`). A scripted remote may carry `ready`. `m6-find` has its own hub on 8797 (`M6_FIND_HUB`).
- `state().hud` (M6b T9): `menu` (open), `bars` (`health`, `energy`, `size`, `life`, each 0..1 as drawn; `life` null while its bar is hidden, as on a practice cell), `chips` (`egg|ready|practice|offline`, in that order) and `hints` (the first-life hint's text on screen, or null). The play screen's DOM: `hud-menu` (the button), `menu` (the panel, where `hud-species`, `hud-biomass`, `hud-energy`, `hud-health`, `hud-growth`, `hud-life`, `hud-stage`, `hud-remaining`, `hud-ready`, `hud-zone`, `menu-net`, `menu-lineage`, `menu-settings`, `menu-controls`, `controls`, `roster` and `menu-build` now sit), `hud-bar-{health,energy,size,life}` (`role="meter"`; the health bar has `data-hurt` while it flashes, M6b T10), the chips `hud-egg`, `hud-mate-ready`, `practice-badge` and `hud-offline`, and `hint`. A test that reads or clicks inside the menu opens it first (`openMenu` in `helpers.ts` presses Esc, which leaves the steering alone); Esc in play opens the menu, not Lineage, once nothing else is open for it to close. `toHaveText` reads a hidden element, so only clicks, screenshots and visibility checks need the menu open. A fresh context gets the first-life hint at the bottom for its first 8 s of play (and another after 30 s); `m6b-hud.spec.ts` uses the M4 hub (8790) for a recorded life's four bars.
- `state().minimap` (M6b T10): `shown`, `radiusWu` (6 view half-heights), `zone`, `depth`, `hazard` (`uv|pressure|null`), `heading` (M6c T12: the heading the arrow shows this frame, radians), `marks` (M6c T12: the round marks the last draw painted, `{ players, pings }` counts; nothing else on the map is round), `players` (`id`, CSS-pixel `x`, `y` inside the minimap, `size` `smaller|comparable|bigger`, `ready`) and `pings` (`id` the sender, `kind`, `x`, `y`), as last drawn (at most 30 times a second). The DOM has `minimap` (the button; a click opens the pond map), `minimap-canvas`, `minimap-arrow` (the player's arrow, an SVG over the canvas whose CSS `transform` turns with the heading every frame), `minimap-depth` (the depth gauge left of the map, `title="Depth"`, with `minimap-depth-marker` ("◂") and `minimap-depth-text` ("540 m": depth × 1,000)), `minimap-zone` (the zone's name) and `minimap-hazard` (`data-hazard`, `svg.uv` or `svg.pressure`, its accessible name and the hidden `hud-zone-warning` say "UV burns" or "Pressure crushes"). `render.fx()` has `shake` (M6c T9: `body` and `camera`, the current offsets in CSS pixels, `kind` `uv|pressure|jolt|null` while a hit settles, and `hits`, the damage hits since hatching, one every 15 ticks at most) and `hurt` (the body's red flash, 0.15 s a hit); `vignette` stays and reads 0, and the `vignette`, `uv-cue`, `pressure-cue` and `hud-depth` elements are gone. Damage happens only while the world runs, so a frozen world never hits; read a kick on the frame that shows it (`polling: 'raf'`). `m6b-minimap` has its own hub on 8802 (`M6B_MINIMAP_HUB`).

## The Lab and the gallery

- `window.__lab` (`src/lab/hook.ts`, always present on `lab.html`): `ready`, `state()` (seed, mode, creatures (each with its `silhouette` class, M6 T8), hovered, selected, parents, offspring), `select(i, { second })` (0-23 the grid, 24-31 the offspring strip), `setMode(mode)` (`genesis` or `evolved`, M6c T5), `renderInfo()`; with `?debug=1` also `debug.{showGenomes(base64url[]), pause(), advance(seconds)}` (replace the grid with those genomes, freeze and step the gait clock for screenshots; a paused Lab also holds the film grain, so a redraw of the same time is pixel-identical, M6 T11). Lab specs measure decoded screenshots of `[data-testid="lab-canvas"]` cell by cell (`cellRect` and `stageCoreRadius` from `src/lab/grid.ts`; helpers in `tests/e2e/lab-helpers.ts`); the page must make no request outside its own origin.
- `window.__gallery` (`src/lab/gallery-page.ts`, on `lab.html?gallery=1`, M6 T5): `ready` (true once the sheet is drawn; it never redraws), `state()` (`width`, `height`, `cols`, `rows`, `radius`, `specimens` with `file`, `comment`, `hash`, `species`, `rarity`, `vertices`, `valid`, and, as drawn in the posed mesh (M6b T3), `organs` (CSS-pixel discs `x`, `y`, `r` of each organ ball and each gut ring), `eyes` (CSS-pixel discs of each eye, M6c T7; the look specs count them as pigment) and `outline` (the body hull, parts excluded, as a CSS-pixel polygon; mask it by the nonzero rule, since a curled loft overlaps itself) and, from a second draw of the part classes (M6c T6, T7, `src/lab/gallery-masks.ts`), `masks`: the cell's room in sheet pixels (`x`, `y`, `w`, `h`) and base64 bitsets over it (decode with `maskBits`): `face`, `clear` (the face 3 px clear of organs), `rim`, `limbs`, `mouth`, `organs` (organ balls clear of parts and glints), `main` (those of the main tone), `glow` (the glowing organs, whole) and `iris`), `renderInfo()`. `lab.html?gallery=1&glow=0` draws the sheet without the organs' glow (`captureGallery(page, baseURL, '&glow=0')`). `glassLook` reads the glass regions (`m6c-glass.spec.ts`, which also places the masks of cells 01–06 on `pass-1.png` and compares their `clear` face); `organLook`, `hueClusters` and `brighterOutside` read the organs' tones, irises and glow (`m6c-organs.spec.ts`, which saves `test-results/m6c-organs-sheet.png`, `-unlit.png` and `-state.json`). `tests/e2e/look-metrics.ts` holds the shared screenshot measures (`solidWhite`, `bodyLook`, `insidePolygon`, `decodePng`); `m6b-look.spec.ts` saves `test-results/m6b-look-sheet.png` and its state. The sheet is fixed at 1600×1000 CSS pixels, pixel ratio 1, high preset, still grain and a fixed gait time; specimens are `tests/fixtures/gallery/*.hex` (a `# comment` line, then hex) in file-name order, and the first 20 are pinned by hash in `src/lab/gallery.test.ts` (karyotype v1 files until rules 8, now stored as their v2 padding, ADR 0032, 0040). `captureGallery` in `tools/gallery-capture.mjs` is shared by `npm run gallery` and `m6-gallery.spec.ts` (in the `post` project).

## Budget scenes

- Firefox (M6c T8): the `firefox` project exists only when `E2E_FIREFOX=1`, which `E2E full`'s firefox part sets after `npx playwright install --with-deps firefox`; PR CI and a plain `test:e2e:full` stay Chromium. It runs `m6c-firefox.spec.ts` (Firefox only, skipped elsewhere: no WebGL console warning beyond the allowlisted texture notices, and the Laplacian energy over each gallery face mask within 1.3× Chromium's, which it launches beside) and `m6c-flash.spec.ts` (`@slow`, Chromium too: 240 frames read back in the page right after the game draws them while the zoom crosses the LOD thresholds, `tests/e2e/crowd.ts` staging 24 bodies at 960×540 on medium). Headless Firefox finds no GL driver on a runner, so it runs headed under Xvfb: `E2E_FIREFOX=1 xvfb-run -a npx playwright test --project=firefox`. In a cloud session install Firefox to a scratch path (`PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=<scratch> npx playwright install firefox`, never `/opt/pw-browsers`) and pass the same variable to the run.
- Budgets (M6 T26): `tools/budget-scene.mjs` stages the budget scenes for both `npm run report:budget` (`docs/perf/budget.md`, screenshots in `test-results/budget/`) and `m6-budget.spec.ts` (`@slow`, in the `post` project): the game at a 1920×1080 drawing buffer, the world frozen, and `empty` (the twilight spawn), `crowded` (the shallows, 24 gallery bodies, a plume, eggs and both size outlines) or `abyss` (the same crowd in the dark). Budgets live in `PRESETS[…].budget` (`calls`, `triangles`, `targetBytes`). The allocation check forces a full GC through the DevTools protocol (`HeapProfiler.collectGarbage`, no `--expose-gc` relaunch) and reads `Runtime.getHeapUsage`.

## Hubs and long scenarios

- Hub E2E: Playwright starts `wrangler dev` on port 8787; open the game with `?hub=http://localhost:8787`. Tests in a file that uses the hub run serially (`test.describe.configure({ mode: 'serial' })`), because the hub fills the fullest pond first. Files run in parallel, so each hub file has its own `wrangler dev` (`playwright.config.ts`: `m2-online` 8787, `m2-social` 8788, `m2-pond` 8789); a new hub file adds a port (`m4-*` share 8790, except `m4-oldage` on 8791 with `LIFESPAN_SCALE=0.01`; `m5-*` on 8793 (`m5-inspect` alone on 8794, `M5_INSPECT_HUB`; `m5-promote` on 8795, `M5_PROMOTE_HUB`; each M5 spec file gets its own hub, since files run in parallel and share a pond otherwise) with `LIFESPAN_SCALE=0.05` and `MATING_SCALE=0.01` (`M5_HUB`); `m4-lifecycle` (`@slow`) on 8792 with `MAX_SESSIONS=2`, `M4_TWO_SEAT_HUB` in `helpers.ts`; every hub starts with `--e2e` so a hatch without a pinned body finds recorded drand rounds). `window.__bs.life.recompute()` returns the round, commit id and DNA hash recomputed from the last genesis, to compare with `tests/fixtures/drand/`, and its record's `rules`; `?genome=` pins a practice cell that never touches the ledger, so a test of the ledger opens the game without it (`openRecorded` in `tests/e2e/helpers.ts`).
- M5 hubs (each spec file its own, `playwright.config.ts`): `m5-court` 8793, `m5-inspect` 8794, `m5-promote` 8795 and `m5-lifecycle` (`@slow`, 8796, `M5_LIFECYCLE_HUB`) with `LIFESPAN_SCALE=0.02` (lives of 1 to 2 min), because the authority credits at most wall time: old age cannot be fast-forwarded past real time. `ripe(page)` runs the sim at 30x until maturity is credited.
- `m6-lifecycle` (`@slow`, M6 T29) has its own hub on 8800 (`M6_LIFECYCLE_HUB`, the M5 hooks) and runs at `?quality=medium` (it clears the low `storageState`): one scenario from the egg reveal to a death in the abyss, asserting `render.info()` budgets and `audio.cues` at every step. `state().player.silhouette` is the body's class (M6 T8).
- `m6b-lifecycle` (`@slow`, M6b T13) has its own hub on 8803 (`M6B_LIFECYCLE_HUB`, the M5 hooks) and runs at `?quality=medium`: the egg at rest and its reveal with nothing over it, the minimal play screen, the speed band (full throttle against the starter's top speed at the same biomass), the wheel held open mid-swim and a ping on the other player's minimap, the UV and pressure shake without an overlay, a death in the abyss and Hatch again, with the medium budgets checked at each step. Its look checks (clipping, colour in the organs) are `m6b-clip` and `m6b-look` in the `post` project; run them with `--project post --no-deps`. The hub ports so far: 8787–8789 (M2), 8790–8792 (M4), 8793–8796 (M5), 8797–8800 (M6), 8801 egg, 8802 minimap and 8803 lifecycle (M6b), 8804 reset, 8805 egg and 8806 lifecycle (M6c), 8807 duel, 8808 HUD, 8809 kill, 8810 carrion, 8811 melee and 8812 lifecycle (M7); the next hub file takes 8813.
- `m6c-lifecycle` (`@slow`, M6c T14) has its own hub on 8806 (`M6C_LIFECYCLE_HUB`, the M5 hooks and the `--e2e` pre-reset hook) and runs at `?quality=medium`: the renewal notice for a staged returning identity, a genesis whose twelve pokes fill the charge and wait for Enter (the still scene, the card's tier equal to the recorded child's), UV in the shallows landing as hits at most every 15 ticks with the camera kick and no overlay, the minimap's depth gauge marker going down from the shallows to the deep, a mating with both carried eggs and the child's hatch, with the medium budgets checked at each step. The look and Firefox checks stay in `m6c-glass`, `m6c-organs`, `m6c-firefox` and `m6c-flash`.
- `state().carrion` (M7 T14): the carrion pieces on the water in this life's world (`id`, `x`, `y`, `value`, `radius`, in id order); `render.info().carrionInstances` counts those drawn (they are not in `foodInstances`). `m7-carrion` has its own hub on 8810 (`M7_CARRION_HUB`): A kills B as in `m7-duel` (the shared steps are in `tests/e2e/m7-fight.ts`: `stage`, `parkBeside`, `fightToEnd`), with `debug.holdBelly(true)` on A (the kill's reward opens room for as much as the pieces hold, so A would eat them before anyone else saw one; the hook holds A's energy over its cap while pieces lie on the water), then the spectator C waits for the same pieces, teleports onto one to eat it, and A's client loses that piece to the claim. `m7-kill` (`@slow`) uses the M5 hooks on 8809 (`M7_KILL_HUB`).
- `m7-lifecycle` (`@slow`, M7 T16) has its own hub on 8812 (`M7_LIFECYCLE_HUB`, `MATING_SCALE` only, so lives keep their normal length) and runs at `?quality=medium`: A and B hatch (A sees the protection shimmer, then none), mate so both carry an egg, B reloads unprotected, and A engages it (`fightToEnd`). Page-side `requestAnimationFrame` watchers record the fight's peaks (the combat HUD shown, and whether every frame stayed within the medium budget), read afterwards. At the end both hashes equal the referee's, and the end reads as the outcome says: a kill names A on B's death screen with no pending child, an eaten egg is told, a survivor keeps its egg.
- Joins and targeting (M7 T15): `state().encounter.aim` is what the target ring marks (`code`: 0–3 a slot, 4–7 that slot's egg; `chosen`: picked with Tab or a click, else the default nearest), null in a plain duel or before the player is seated; a joiner's `slot` is -1 until its `enc-join` arrives, and it swims free until its frame begins. `render.info().telegraphs.targets` counts the target rings drawn this frame. Tab is a combat key in a fight (the page keeps it from the browser's focus), and a click on a fighter or an egg targets it. `m7-melee` has its own hub on 8811 (`M7_MELEE_HUB`): A engages B as in `m7-duel`, while C, parked on B's far side, presses F in its own page the moment B's snapshot shows it fighting (a hatched body can finish B within seconds), joins as the third fighter, and presses Tab; then A and C swim off in opposite directions until the fight ends, and every client's hash equals the referee's.
- `m7-duel` (M7 T11) has its own hub on 8807 (`M7_DUEL_HUB`): two players hatch at once, B reloads and resumes (a resume carries no spawn protection, a genesis 15 s), A parks beside B until `attack.hint` names B and presses F, sets its stance in the page's frame that first shows the fight, and follows B with `debug.steerTo({ id })` until `encounter.over`. `render.fx().engage` counts the engages sent, each with its ring (counted in the key press itself). The DOM has `attack-hint` (the "F" glyph), `shield-shimmer` (one per protected remote, inside `attack-marks`), `inspect-attack` (the card's Attack button, disabled with the reason in `inspect-attack-why`) and the refusals in `toast`.
- `m7-hud` (M7 T12) has its own hub on 8808 (`M7_HUD_HUB`): a duel like `m7-duel` with no egg, where B turns on `debug.autoBrace(true)` (client inputs only: the fight input braces whenever a windup aimed at the player or its egg has 4 ticks or fewer left as its frame begins). `state().encounter` adds, per fighter, `radius`, `staggered` and `outside` (ticks outside every opponent's escape range), and `hud`: the combat HUD (`shown`, `stance`, `brace` `{ ready, cooldown, active }`, `opponents` `{ player, health }` by slot, `escape` 0..1) and the fight's cues so far (`windups` aimed at the player, `blocks`, `hits`). `render.info().telegraphs` has the marks drawn this frame (`glows`, `sparks`, `rings`, and `targets` from M7 T15), the `flashes` and `wobbles` running, and the totals since load (`flashed`, `sparked`, `wobbled`). `state().audio.cues` counts `windup`, `block`, `kill` and `egg-eaten`. The DOM has `combat-hud` with `stance-aggressive`, `stance-balanced`, `stance-evasive` (`aria-pressed` on the current one), `brace` (`data-ready`, `data-active`) and its `brace-cooldown` ring, and the `combat-marks` SVG layer; `body[data-fighting]` hides the size and life bars, and `hudCover(page, extra)` in `helpers.ts` is the HUD budget's measure. A frozen world (`timeScale(0)`) may still play a backlog of ticks, so the scenario shoots only once a frame passes without a tick.
- The world renewal (M6c T2): `state().renewed` is what the hub's last `life` said (true at most once per identity, null until it answered) and the DOM has `renewed`, the title's notice. `wrangler dev` always starts migrated, so `m6c-reset.spec.ts` (its own hub on 8804, `M6C_RESET_HUB`) stages a returning player with the E2E entry's hook: `POST /v1/e2e/pre-reset` with `{ playerId }` sets its `first_seen` just before the reset and `epoch_seen` to 0.
