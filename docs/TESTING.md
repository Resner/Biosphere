# Testing reference

The test hooks' API, the budget scenes and the hub ports. The rules for tests are in `.claude/rules/tests.md`; this is the reference they point to. Read the parts your test uses, and update this file in the same PR when you change a hook or add a hub.

## The game (`window.__bs`)

- `window.__bs` (`src/game/debug.ts`): `ready`, `state()` (screen, `zone` and `depth` under the player (null on the title; M6 T16), quality (`setting`, `detected` the preset auto resolves to, `bench` `pending|running|measured|stored|skipped`: automation is `skipped`, a stored result for this GPU `stored`), tick, player, death (`cause`, `survivedSeconds`, `peakBiomass`, `body`, and `by`, the killer's name when the hub settled a kill; M7 T13), life (the recorded life: `status` is the client state machine `idle|requesting|awaiting|verifying|playing|dead|practice`, plus `creature`, `genome`, `activeMs`, `lifespanMs`, `awaitingBeacon`, `lastAckN`, `ackedBiomass` (the biomass in the report the hub last took), `sentBiomass` (the most in any of the last reports sent, acked or not), `genesisIn`, `practice`, `stage`, `remainingMs`, `limitMs`), mate (`state`, `text` the line on screen, `invite`, `visited` states, `ready`), offspring (`kind`, `creature`, `species`, `rarity`, `generation`, `genomeHash`, `cardShown`, `eggShown`, `options` the ids of the title and death choices), remotes (each with `radius`, `genomeHash` and `species`, null while the tinted cell stands in), world (`id`, `seed`, `size` the world's side in wu, `centre` its spawn centre, null with no world; M11 T12: specs place things relative to `centre`, never at absolute coordinates; M11 T14: there is no roster), social, net (`dropped` counts genome events ignored, `signed` the peers whose verified signed snapshot is held, `badSignatures` the signed snapshots that failed to verify, `heldTicks` the tick of each peer's newest held one by player id; M7 T7), encounter (the current or last refereed fight, null before one, M7 T10: `id`, own `slot`, `frame` simulated here, `confirmed` frames covered by the referee's bundles from frame 0, `over` once `enc-end` arrived, `hash` the local `hashEncounter` then, `rollbacks`, `resyncs`, `desyncs`, and `fighters` by slot with `player`, `creature`, simulated `x`, `y`, `heading`, `health`, `maxHealth`, `stance`, `windup` (ticks left or null), `target` (the windup's target code or null), `braced`, `egg` (`x`, `y`, `health`, `eaten`, or null) and `outcome` (`in` while fighting)); M7 T11: `encounter.endHash` is the referee's hash from `enc-end` (null before it), each of `remotes` has `protected` and `fighting` (its snapshot's marks), and `attack` is `{ hint, shimmer, waiting, refusal }`: the remote the "F" glyph marks (the nearest this player could engage now) or null, the remotes drawn with the protection shimmer, whether an engage awaits its answer, and why the last one went unsent (`unsigned`) or was refused (`engage:<why>`), else null; `player` includes `speed`, `elder`, `genomeHash`, `species`, `rarity`, `tier` and the sim's `stats`).
- Also on `window.__bs`: `events()` (counts since hatching), `replay(log)` (hash of an input log), `replayEncounter(log)` (an encounter log's final `hash` and `results`), `goldens` (always present, it only computes: `sets()` names the golden sets and `run(set, input)` recomputes one in the page's own bundle, loading `src/game/goldens.ts` on first use; `tests/goldens-catalogue.ts` holds each set's input and the output its golden files pin, and `m14-goldens.spec.ts` compares the two for every set in each engine), `render.info()` (`remoteCells` fallback cells, `remoteBodies` genome bodies; `preset`, `pixelRatio`, `particles`; the post chain's `passes`, `targets`, `targetBytes`, `renderScale` and `dof` (`none|half|full`); the player body's `lod` (`high|medium|low`, null without a body) and mesh `vertices` (M6 T10); `caustic` (the caustics' strength at the camera) and `rays` (the god rays' strength drawn, always 0 on low; M6 T17); `outlines` (`{ bigger, smaller }`, the size outlines drawn, M6 T21); `calls` and `triangles` count the whole frame, every post pass included), `render.outlines()` (the size outlines as last drawn, M6 T21: `id`, `kind` `bigger|smaller`, CSS-pixel centre `x`, `y` and `radius`, `pulse` 0..1, always 0 for `smaller`), `render.specks(layer)` (`far|mid|near`: each on-screen speck's `index`, CSS-pixel `x`, `y`, `size`, depth `z`, CoC `blur` and `bright` (one of high's bright specks, M6 T17), as last drawn).
- With `?debug=1` also `debug.{setEnergy, setBiomass, setHealth, teleport, netCap, crowdAt, progressNow, setZone, nearestFood(kind?), steerTo, hover, spawnPlume, setRemotes, joinWorld, deliver, hasFood, timeScale, freezeAt, resetWater, setClock, setBench}`; `freezeAt(tick, x, y)` freezes the world (as `timeScale(0)`) at a sim tick with the player stopped at a place and the camera on it, so a shot does not depend on how many ticks a slow runner played first, nor on a backlog of ticks the loop held at the freeze, which it drops (`m6-dof`, `m11-world`); `setZone(zone)` teleports the player to the nearest point well inside that zone (clear of its edge bands, its surround in the zone too) and returns it (M6 T16); freeze the world first (`timeScale(0)`) where UV or pressure would hurt; `setBench(ms)` replaces every measured frame time with `ms` and re-runs the auto-detect benchmark at once (8 high, 15 medium, 30 low), and adaptive resolution follows it, which is otherwise pinned at 1 under automation (low folds `renderScale` into `pixelRatio`); `steerTo` returns a promise, and works in a fight; its `id` may name a remote player, whose drawn body it follows until touching it (M7 T11); `setHealth(hp)` sets the player's health on this client only, never above its maximum, and peers and the hub see it in the snapshots; `timeScale(0)` also holds the shaders' animation clock, so specks stand still for screenshots; `resetWater()` zeroes the marine snow's flow and, once frozen, the shaders' clock (M6 T17), so two frozen pages hold the same specks. `setClock(seconds)` sets a frozen world's shaders' clock and leaves the flow, and the caustics' and god rays' clock (`SunClock`) re-bases to it at the camera's pace, so a frame at a set clock is the same on every run (M6b T8, `m6b-water.spec.ts`: each zone measured from the pond's centre at clock 100). `?dof=0` draws every preset without its depth of field and `?light=0` without caustics or god rays (test switches). `teleport` (and so `setZone` and `freezeAt`) fills the zones' depth window at once, so the next frame shows the new place's zones (M11 T11).
- The world at full size (M11 T11): `?world=<size>` (8,192 to 262,144 wu in steps of 4,096) sizes the offline local world, which spawns at its centre. `render.info()` adds `origin` (the floating origin the scene draws from, wu) and `centre` (the player's centre as the GPU draws it, CSS pixels, from the 32-bit positions it is sent; null with no player). The test switch `?gpu=watch` wraps the WebGL context's float uploads, and `render.gpu()` returns `{ max, name, uploads }`: the largest |float| sent so far in a uniform or a vertex buffer, the uniform that sent it (`buffer` for vertex data), and the uploads seen (null without the switch). The look across tiers (M11 T17): `render.info()` adds `look` (the look's scale: the view's half-height over a starter adult's, never below 1) and `swarms` (the swarm clouds drawn, fading ones included); `state()`'s session swarm is not exposed, only what is drawn. `m11-world.spec.ts` (no hub) checks the water's zone colours against a pond of 8,192 wu (`@slow`), a still creature's `centre` over 30 frames, the medium budgets and `render.gpu()` at the centre, at (250,000, 250,000) and by the walls, and saves `test-results/m11-world-centre.png` and `m11-world-far.png`.

## The Lab and the gallery
